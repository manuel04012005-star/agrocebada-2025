Barley yield forecast for the 59 blind parcels, 2025 season

A model trained on the 138 parcels with known yield in Hidalgo, Puebla and Tlaxcala. It builds on our exploratory analysis of the Basic (Sentinel‑2, Landsat) and PRO (Planet) datasets, and adds CHIRPS/CHIRTS climate and INEGI CEM topography.

01 · Map

Parcels by yield

How to use the map
  • Read the colors: darker green means higher yield (t/ha). Circles are the 138 training parcels (observed yield); squares with a gold border are the 59 blind parcels (forecast yield).
  • Filter: use All, Blind only or Training only on the right to highlight one group; the rest fade out.
  • Jump to a zone: the municipality buttons at the top of the map (Chignahuapan, Calpulalpan, Singuilucan…) zoom straight to that group of parcels.
  • Move and zoom: drag the map to pan; use the mouse wheel or the + and − buttons to zoom; ⌂ returns to the full view. Zoom in to see each parcel's real outline.
  • See details: hover over a parcel to see its ID and yield; click it to open its card on the right with area, elevation, slope, 2025 rain and its NDVI curve compared with the average.
